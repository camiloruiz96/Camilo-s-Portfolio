HOW I WORK

I don’t start with the feature.

“I don’t pretend to have all the answers. I ask enough questions to find the problem that is actually worth solving — and once we find it, I like to build.”

01

Find the real problem.

A request is evidence of pain, not necessarily evidence of the right solution.

02

Make it tangible.

Prototype enough to learn before asking engineering to make the expensive investment.

03

Measure behaviour.

Outcomes matter: time saved, friction removed, engagement improved and behaviour changed.

04

Think beyond go-live.

Good products need maintainability, governance, adoption and operational sustainability.

PRODUCT × TECHNOLOGY × DELIVERY

Technical enough to go deep. Product-minded enough to know why.

I work best between users, business and technical teams — turning ambiguous problems into something that can be tested, learned from and eventually scaled.
